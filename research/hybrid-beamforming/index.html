Steering beams and spatial notches

Hybrid beamforming combines radio-frequency and baseband processing to support multiple data streams without assigning a complete digital chain to every antenna element.

Our 28-GHz transmitter integrates spatial notch steering with hybrid beamforming to enable concurrent dual data streams for MIMO applications. The CICC 2021 demonstration and JSSC 2024 study connect circuit implementation to measured spatial behavior.
