RISE Lab

Welcome

Welcome to the Rice Integrated Systems and Electromagnetics (RISE) Lab. Our research focuses on developing integrated circuits and systems, along with their design automation methodologies, for high-speed communication, sensing, and healthcare applications.

We have immediate openings for self-motivated and creative Ph.D. students, postdocs, and visitors who are interested in (1) Agentic AI and AI-assisted design automation for analog/RF integrated circuits, (2) RF/mmWave/sub-THz beamformers, transceivers, and antenna arrays, (3) high-speed wireline circuits, and (4) analog/mixed-signal integrated circuits for brain-machine interfaces. Please contact Dr. Chi directly with your CV.

Email: taiyun.chi AT rice.edu

Recent news

All news ↗
July 2026
Houbo and Chris’s paper titled “FoundaRF: A Physics-Informed Foundation Model Framework for Radio-Frequency Passive Components” has been accepted to ICCAD 2026. This paper presents a foundation model framework that unifies passive layout modeling across diverse layout structures and technologies. Congratulations Houbo, Chris, and our collaborators at UT Austin!
June 2026
Our tutorial paper titled "Doherty Power Amplifiers: Fundamentals and Recent Advances" has been accepted to IEEE Solid-State Circuits Mag. (SSC-M). This paper is adapted from Dr. Chi's 2026 ISSCC tutorial, offering a comprehensive review of the Doherty PA, from its foundational principles to the latest silicon implementations.
May 2026
Yueqi and Houbo’s paper titled “RF-Agent: A Practical Framework for Building Language Agents for RFIC Design” has been accepted to ICLAD 2026. Congratulations!
March 2026
Our preprint on a high-channel-count neural interface is now online. We developed an ASIC featuring 5,376 simultaneous recording channels, in-pixel amplification, full-bandwidth recording (20 kS/s per channel), multiplexed ADCs (12 bits), and on-chip stimulation. In collaboration with Prof. Chong Xie’s group, we demonstrated high-density flexible probe integration and in vivo recordings in rats. Congratulations to Yingying, who led this multi-year effort spanning chip design, chip-probe integration, system miniaturization, and animal experiments!
Update in August 2026: This manuscript has been accepted to npj Biomedical Innovations.
February 2026
Hao and Yaolong’s ISSCC 2025 paper received the ISSCC Lewis Winner Outstanding Paper Award. Hao is also a co-first author of our ISSCC 2024 paper that won the same award. Congratulations on this back-to-back achievement!
